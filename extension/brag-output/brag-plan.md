# TextOrigin AI — Brag Plan

**Tone preset:** `app-store` — smooth, feature-card clean, corporate but not boring.
**Creative direction:** Quiet premium product film — the serious side of AI detection, delivered with polish.

## 9-question rubric

1. **What is the app?** TextOrigin AI is a Chrome extension that detects AI-generated text by identifying stylistic markers in user-selected content, returning a scored analysis with confidence level.

2. **What is the funniest or most impressive claim?** "Identifiez les marqueurs stylistiques des textes générés par l'IA" — it claims to see the invisible fingerprints that LLMs leave on every sentence.

3. **What is the visual hook?** The floating purple (#4f46e5) "Analyze" button on any webpage, plus the colored highlight markers (orange, purple, blue, yellow) that paint the detected text. The popup score card with big typography is also striking.

4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?** The popup with the AI Marker Score, the floating button in action, and the highlighted text on the page — all three should appear on screen.

5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?** 18 seconds — select text (2s), click Analyze (2s), score reveal with highlights (8s), confidence tag + outro (6s).

6. **What tone fits best?** `app-store` preset. The product is earnest and utility-first; the video should feel like an Apple-style feature spot — clean typography, restrained motion, confident delivery.

7. **What should the audio feel like?** Polished audio layer: a warm, minimal music bed (no chaos) with subtle UI interaction SFX — button clicks, score reveal, toast slide-in. No voiceover needed.

8. **What should the share caption say?** "Can you tell which sentence was written by AI? TextOrigin AI knows. Install the extension and find out."

9. **What's the user flow worth showing?** Entry: user selects text on any page. Key action: clicks the floating Analyze button. Result: score appears with highlighted markers and confidence level.
